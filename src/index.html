Conversion Model Lab

Is the model ready for a controlled pilot? Upload the test-set predictions to see ranking quality, stability over time, where the model fails, and how model targeting compares with today's outreach.

Drop one or more CSVs of test predictions
One row per client and prediction date. Upload several files to switch between them — one is analysed at a time. Parquet also works.
Processed locally in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Required columns

Matched by name (case-insensitive); you confirm or change the mapping next.

  • CLIENT_IDClient identifier.
  • DATE_TPrediction (as-of) date. Also matches date.
  • CONVERSION_TARGETConverted within the outcome horizon (default 14 days) after DATE_T. Also matches y_true.
  • PREDICTED_SCOREModel score used for ranking. Also matches y_score, score_raw.

Optional columns

Each unlocks specific analyses. Everything else is analysed automatically as a segment or feature.

  • PREDICTED_PROBABILITYCalibrated probability → calibration analysis.
  • PRIMARY_STUDIO_IDStudio segment and per-studio caps.
  • PURCHASE_DATE / DAYS_TO_CONVERSIONExact purchase dates for campaign-level counting.
  • CURRENT_STRATEGY_FLAGToday's targeting, for a like-for-like comparison.
  • SPLIT · MODEL_VERSION · TREATMENT_GROUPTest isolation, version filter, experiments.
